## Consequence for the current port

The tall developer lift currently advances its kinematic pose into a fragment already blocked by the ceiling. The energy fix is valid but cannot stop that pose from passing through the fragment. None of the tested fragment contact paths supplies a deletion or crush-damage rule that would resolve it.

Next implementation should examine mover admission and blocked-push handling before committing transforms. A port policy may limit an obstructed mover, but it must be labelled as such, account for all affected fragments, preserve controller/attached poses and interval consistency, and validate rollback or partial motion against static world and other movers. Do not discard only the failing fragment or roll back one mover after unrelated bodies have already been published. Keep the0.5-unit carry, tipping, withdrawal, finite obstacle and retained1.5-unit ceiling cases as controls. Actor-specific crushing remains a separate gameplay integration requirement.
