# Campaign undercover first pass

The five authored `Go_Undercover` events (type 47) now have a gameplay handler. The event's first word selects the form: zero is `parker_suit`, and nonzero is `parker_sci` in the original executable. The original dispatch and entry/exit services are at `4bcdb0`, `4bcdc0`, `4b00f0`, and `4b0380`; binary-derived contracts and the original executable hash are in `docs/research/FUTURE-CAMPAIGN-AI-MODE-UNDERCOVER-20260915.md` and its entry, exit, and identity follow-ups.

On entry the port requests the distinct `Undercover 12mm handgun` resource from the level's event demand, grants and selects that weapon, remembers the previous selection, clears current and class armor, clears player flag `0x40` at `0x810`, and sets bit `0x10000` at `0x7d0`. The player body and health remain live. Ordinary unalerted guards do not newly acquire an uncompromised disguised player; pre-existing/scripted combat continues, and firing marks the disguise compromised. On event OFF the port restores the normal class armor and both flags, then tries to restore the previous selection. The form is carried in memory for level transitions, and a death restart clears it. These are first-pass gameplay approximations: the current player entity and camera are not recreated as the original does, and the full inventory constructor/transfer contract is not established.

The ordinary environment checkpoint now writes RFEN4 with a 24-byte player-form record after the cutscene record. It stores activity, exact authored variant, compromise state, previous weapon slot and unarmed state, and the normal class armor. Preparation validates these fields and older RFEN1/2/3 saves still decode as no active form. The loader admits the undercover weapon resource and ownership before publication, restores the form and flags with the player inventory, and retains it for a later in-memory section transition.
