# Campaign cutscene resources and runtime, first pass

The shared PC/Xbox core now owns bounded v180 level sections `0x400` (cameras), `0x4000` (timelines), `0x5000` (control points), and `0x6000` (paths). It decodes the original 16-bit length-prefixed strings, reordered camera basis, descriptor selectors/FOV/hide flag, ordered point durations and words, and four-control cubic paths. Camera and path joins are validated before publication; a failed load leaves the previous owner unchanged. One allocation holds the retained records, while the temporary source sections are charged to the caller's peak budget and released after loading. Source references: [timeline loader research](research/FUTURE-CAMPAIGN-CUTSCENE-TIMELINES-20260915.md) and [camera/path arithmetic](research/FUTURE-CAMPAIGN-CUTSCENE-CAMERA-PATHS-20260915.md).

The installed-level check loads all 12 authored cutscene levels and all 84 timeline points, including L6S3 UID 3696's 14 points, 15 cameras and two paths. Its `pleasework` midpoint samples within 0.0005 of the original-code result. The focused PC check and the NXDK image build pass.

The first runtime pass now dispatches event type 55 into a scene-owned cutscene clock, installs the selected camera, advances authored points and cubic paths, fires each point's linked action, masks player input, and restores normal camera/input when the timeline finishes. Event type 83 receives the completion pulse. L6S3's opening point starts both authored actor animations and its sound chain. Their RFA payloads total 1,085,298 bytes, so the bounded resident-motion ceiling is now 1,280 KiB; this allocates on demand, and the stock-memory XEMU run had 5,139 physical pages (20.07 MiB) available at its endpoint.

L6S3 UID 3696 completed a 300-frame neutral replay on PC and stock 64 MiB XEMU. The inspected native framebuffer shows the authored room and Gryphon and matches the PC frame. A later 3,060-frame PC replay traversed the full 47.82-second authored timeline: all 14 point actions fired, the type 83 completion chain restored the three disabled navpoints, teleported the player, started Gryphon's movement, and displayed `You lead the way.`. The cutscene became inactive with zero recorded errors. Sixty frames of post-completion forward input moved the player from `(1.165927,-4.118478,-23.027193)` to `(4.426909,-4.118478,-19.424313)`. This proves PC control restoration for that staged path; the long walking fixture also re-enters an authored trigger because it staged the cutscene early.

The authored 45-degree camera FOV now scales world projection after combat's weapon-scope update; gameplay HUD/reticle hide during the cutscene while subtitles remain available. The inspected PC and XEMU opening frames show the same Gryphon close-up instead of the former wide gameplay view. A stock 64 MiB XEMU replay of the complete 3,060-frame sequence passes the shared state checks, including exact 12-word `CUTSCENE`, animation and scripted movement state. Its inspected final frame matches PC's open-door Gryphon handoff and subtitle; 5,061 physical pages (19.77 MiB) remained available. The Xbox run used neutral input, so post-cutscene movement is directly confirmed on PC and native hands-on movement remains open. Camera look-at, skip/cancel behavior, other authored sequences, save/load during a cutscene, and broader timing/parity review remain.

The teleport placement adapter now uses the original `4a0770` full-body sweep: from 0.25 units above the authored destination to 2 units below, with 0.05 units of clearance on a hit. Query flags come from the body state (`state_124|4`, plus `0x100` for a tiny body), and the scene collision facade includes committed movers. A 3,000-frame L6S3 PC replay after this change completed normally and remained supported at `(1.165927,-4.121857,-23.027193)` through frame 2999. The NXDK image builds; native replay of this particular placement change is still open.
