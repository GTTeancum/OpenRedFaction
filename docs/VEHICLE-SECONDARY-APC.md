# Independent secondary APC ownership

The first independent vehicle owner is an unoccupied, ungrouped APC receiving an authored `Goto`. It retains the existing passive registry handle, damage state, chassis resources and rendering owner. One level-owned record supplies rigid physics, suspension and navigation separately from the selected player vehicle. Three bounded stock-64-MiB cloud Xbox phases now pass, including ordinary save/fresh-load and retirement continuity.

## Runtime contract

- Admit one eligible authored APC with exact registry/damage identity and a genuine parsed `Goto` targeting its UID exactly once. Another owner cannot steal the slot. Existing seats, group attachment and riders prevent admission; a later rider pauses the body without changing support ownership.
- Advance once per actual world step, after controllers clear passive motion projections and before ordinary actor updates. Frame zero does not integrate the new body. The selected solver retains its existing order, including its final render-endpoint step.
- Use the existing navigation graph request and a four-node retained prefix. Start/goal storage belongs to the owner; exhausting a prefix requests another graph segment. There is no direct-destination fallback through walls. Steering tolerances and speed reductions are shared first-pass driving policy, not a claim of original executable parity.
- Step a private rigid/navigation/support candidate and publish pose matrices and accepted displacement velocity only after successful collision. Keep that contact velocity distinct from authoritative rigid velocity. No group-attachment or rider-interval flag is fabricated.
- Compose ordinary world/GeoMod/movers, props/fragments, player/NPC/turret bodies and other visible vehicle hulls using the querying chassis handle. The selected and secondary bodies use the same provider while the independent owner exists. They step sequentially against current committed poses; no coupled impulse solver or crush response is introduced.
- `Goto` OFF stops the matching order; completed and stopped orders remain owned. Vehicle promotion continues to reject that owner. Scripted physics freeze/wake updates its actual rigid state.
- Ordinary `Remove_Object` stops and retires the independent owner without damage effects or registry replacement. Its hidden/dead object bits suppress live presentation and contacts; its separate retirement state reports absent to `When_Dead`. Authored vitals remain intact. Later `UnHide` and `Goto` cannot revive it.

## Ordinary saves

Optional `RFSV1` wraps `RFVA2` beneath `RFPV`. Its explicit 256-byte little-endian row stores UID/profile, retirement, full mutable rigid values, scripted physics flags, authored Goto identity/target, retained node ordinals and cursor/retry/cost, start/goal positions, copied vitals/flags, and the distinct accepted contact velocity. Resources and inertia come from the exact class. Handles and pointers are rebuilt; navigation search scratch is temporary and is regenerated by the next request.

Preparation rejects invalid owner/event bindings, nonfinite/nonrigid state, malformed route ordinals and cross-component disagreements with RFVA/RFPV. Candidate placement checks staged world/movers/props, the player, unrelated NPCs, selected chassis and all other visible passive hulls. No rider or authored-overlap exemption is introduced. Assignment occurs after all validation and rebinds both collision providers. Legacy payloads without RFSV remain readable and clear session-local independent state without replaying an event.

A native opt-in audit exercises exact roundtrip, legacy passthrough, wrong UID/profile/event, invalid float/basis/node/lifecycle/reserved data, component disagreement, budget/truncation and unchanged live ownership. This is decoder/admission compatibility evidence; it is not a separate legacy gameplay replay.
