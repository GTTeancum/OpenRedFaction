# NPC firearm interception

Ordinary aimed NPC bullets and shotgun pellets previously tested only their intended actor. The separate fixed-point Shoot_At path scanned nearest bodies, but could miss an outstretched shield on another actor. With allied combat active this allowed shots to pass through a nearer living actor.

All non-melee NPC hitscan rays and individual shotgun pellets now use `scene_ai_actor_ray_select` after spread sampling. Each generation-valid living player/NPC candidate combines its physical body with its independent held-shield query; the nearest candidate owns contact regardless of affiliation. Source actors and actual seat/operator/generated ownership pairs are excluded. Hidden, removed and dead actors are excluded. The selector allocates no memory and changes no AI state.

Existing vehicle, clutter, fragment and world cover arbitration clips to the selected contact. Shield commit and ordinary damage/death paths remain authoritative; a rejected shield commit only falls back to a real body hit after checking cover to that body's fraction. The AI's intended target is retained separately for pursuit, target completion and Attack health telemetry. Damage attribution follows the actual hit actor. Melee/projectiles and railgun world-penetration policy are unchanged.

`rf_scene_enemy_actor_hits` retains up to16 actual damaging contacts with source/intended/actual UIDs, frame, damage and actual/intended health. It is diagnostic only and resets per scene. The bounded test uses ordinary Attack against a target behind an intervening neutral actor, followed by ordinary blocker removal.
