# Combat while driving or using vehicle seats

The common combat update returned immediately whenever a player occupied a vehicle. This stopped NPC and stationary-turret attacks, existing player/NPC projectile lifetimes, and burning damage. It affected normal authored encounters: L1S2 includes a Driller and hostile guards, and L1S3 includes APC9627 and hostile guards.

The shared once-per-frame combat update now continues during possession. Player equipment inputs are separately gated: no handheld firing, weapon cycling, scope/scanner activation, shield bash, nearby pickup collection or developer refill while seated. Existing projectiles, explosions, burns and independent enemy attacks retain their ordinary lifetime and damage paths. Fusion and Machine Pistol selection also observe the possession gate. Held riot-shield collision is disabled while mounted, preventing an invisible handheld shield from blocking incoming bullets. Vehicle input remains owned by its existing control path.
